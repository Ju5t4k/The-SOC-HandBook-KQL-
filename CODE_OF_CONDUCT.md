# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Argue about the query,
not about the person who wrote it.

## What this looks like in practice

This is a repository about detecting and investigating attacks, which means
people will disagree about tradeoffs — thresholds, false positive rates,
whether a signal is worth the noise. That disagreement is the point. Have it
about the evidence.

**Encouraged**
- "This will fire on every Citrix estate, here is why" — specific, checkable
- "I tested this on a 40k-seat tenant and it timed out at 30d"
- Saying you do not know, or that you have not tested something
- Correcting your own earlier answer without ceremony

**Not welcome**
- Personal attacks, insults, or dismissing someone based on their experience
  level. The person asking the beginner question is the person this repository
  is for.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Publishing anyone's private information
- Gatekeeping — "if you don't know that you shouldn't be in security"

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and commit messages in this
repository.

## Enforcement

Report to the repository owner at
[akwiatkowski75@gmail.com](mailto:akwiatkowski75@gmail.com). Reports are
handled privately. Outcomes range from a request to edit a comment through to a
block, depending on severity and whether it is repeated.
